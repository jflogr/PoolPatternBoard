How to use the board

Lay out a rack, then plan how you would run it: where each ball goes, where the cue ball should land for the next shot, and how it gets there. The table is a 9 ft table to scale (100 × 50 in cloth, 2.25 in balls).

1. Put the balls on the table

  • Drag a ball from the tray onto the table, or tap it in the tray and then tap the table.
  • Drag a ball to move it. Drag it off the table, double-click it, or select it and press Delete to put it back in the tray.
  • Share layout makes a link to the balls alone, with no pockets, zones or lines. Anyone who opens it gets the same rack to plan their own way.
  • A shared layout opens with the balls locked, so planning can't move one by accident. Locked balls can still be selected and planned. 🔒 Balls locked (by Balls and Lines) unlocks them; 🔓 Lock balls locks your own layout too.

2. Plan each ball

Click a ball to select it. The bar under the buttons shows what it has, and its × buttons clear each part.

  • Pocket: click a pocket. A dotted line runs from the back of the ball, away from the pocket: the line the cue ball is on for a straight-in shot. Click the pocket again to clear it.
  • Set zone (Z): where the cue ball should end up. A target ring goes there, with a good zone around it along the line to the ball.
  • Widen (W): outside the good zone, stretches the OK zone out to that spot. Use it on more than one side to grow the OK zone further.
  • Movement (M): the cue ball's path from the ghost ball (pick the pocket first). Click a rail for each bounce, then click the cloth where the cue ball stops.
  • Draw zone (G): draw a good zone by hand, one corner at a time. Click the first corner again, double-click or press Enter to close it.

With a mouse, the keys act where the mouse is, so keep it over the table. The buttons (shown on phones and tablets) work the other way round: tap the button, then tap the table.

3. Fine-tune

  • Click a zone to show its points. Drag a point to move it, or click it and nudge it with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps).
  • Click a zone's edge to add a point there; drag an edge to slide that side.
  • Double-click a point, or select it and press Delete, to remove it.
  • Drag the target ring, or the points of a movement path. Bounces stay on the rails.

4. Lines

Switch to Lines (L) to draw free lines: drag across the table, or click the start and then the end. Click a line to move it, drag its ends, or change its color, width and arrow. Balls (B) goes back.

Showing and saving

  • The Show checkboxes under the tray hide pocket lines (P), targets, zones or movement.
  • Save image downloads a PNG of the table.
  • Your board saves itself in this browser. Undo (Ctrl+Z) and Redo (Ctrl+Y) cover every change, even Clear table.

On a phone

  • Held upright, the table stands on end so it's bigger. Turn the phone sideways for the usual view.
  • Use the buttons above the table for Set zone, Widen, Movement and the rest.
  • Double-tap a ball or a point to remove it, or select it and tap Delete.
